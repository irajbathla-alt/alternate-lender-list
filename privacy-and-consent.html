VANCOUVER FINANCE COMPANY INC.

Privacy & Consent

Version 1.2 · Effective November 25, 2026

We collect information to assess and arrange financing at your request. Relevant information may be shared with suitable financing providers with your authorization. Credit inquiries may affect your credit score. Marketing is optional. You may contact us to access, correct or withdraw consent to the use of your information, subject to applicable law.

1. Scope and accountability

Vancouver Finance Company Inc. (VFC, we, us) is responsible for personal information under its control. This policy covers inquiries, eligibility assessments, applications, client and member services, and communications. We aim to comply with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where applicable, and other applicable provincial privacy, consumer reporting and electronic commerce laws. Our Privacy Officer is our point of contact for privacy matters.

2. Information we collect

Depending on the service, we may collect names, contact details, addresses, date of birth, identification, business ownership and registration details, industry, time in business, revenue, requested financing, estimated credit range, financial statements, bank statements, tax records, income, assets, liabilities, credit reports, property information, existing financing, correspondence, signatures and application records. Website and service providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, authentication records and technical logs. We request only information reasonably necessary for the identified purpose. A social insurance number is not a general condition of service; if requested for a specific lawful purpose, we will explain why and whether it is optional.

Additional information may include credit card processing statements, sales activity, expenses, lease details, account balances, payment behaviour, collections, judgments, bankruptcies, consumer proposals, credit inquiries and loan statements, where reasonably necessary and lawfully obtained.

3. Sources and purposes

Information may come from you, your authorized representatives, credit reporting agencies, financial institutions, financing providers, public registries and verification services, with appropriate consent or other lawful authority. We use it to respond to inquiries, assess eligibility and affordability, verify identity and documents, identify suitable providers, prepare and submit authorized applications, communicate decisions and conditions, support transactions, maintain accounts and records, improve services using appropriately minimized information, prevent fraud, resolve disputes and satisfy legal obligations.

Authorized financing purposes include packaging, submitting, supporting and managing applications for loans, refinancing, merchant cash advances, working capital and equipment financing; assisting providers with underwriting, risk assessment, closing, renewals, servicing and lawful collection-related requirements where relevant to the authorized transaction. Information may also be used to protect legitimate rights and interests where permitted by law.

4. Financing and credit authorization

When you separately give informed authorization in connection with an application, you authorize VFC to collect, use and disclose necessary information to assess and arrange the financing you request, and to verify relevant information with authorized sources. Before obtaining or arranging a personal credit report, we will seek the express authorization required by applicable law and explain the purpose. Prospective lenders may require their own authorization and may conduct independent inquiries. Hard inquiries may affect your credit score. Any subsequent inquiry must be justified by the authorized purpose and applicable law; new authorization will be sought when required. An eligibility inquiry or merely visiting this page does not authorize a credit pull.

Credit bureau consent: By separately signing or affirmatively accepting the applicable credit authorization, I authorize Vancouver Finance Company Inc. and prospective financing providers identified in connection with my application to obtain and review my personal and applicable business credit reports from Equifax, TransUnion or other authorized credit reporting agencies for financing assessment, verification and underwriting. I understand that one or more hard credit inquiries may be made and may lower my credit score. Different providers may make separate inquiries. Authorization is limited to the stated purpose; further consent will be obtained where required by law.

5. Disclosure and independent providers

With appropriate authorization, we may share relevant information with prospective banks, credit unions, private and alternative lenders, financing intermediaries, credit bureaus, verification providers and professional advisers involved in your request. Service providers may process information for hosting, email, document storage, electronic signatures, authentication and administration. We remain accountable for information processed on our behalf and use appropriate contractual or other safeguards. Independent lenders assess applications under their own privacy policies and may retain information under their own legal obligations. You may ask which providers receive your information. We do not sell personal information. Without consent, information is collected, used or disclosed only where permitted or required by law, such as a lawful order or a legally authorized fraud investigation.

Recipients may include merchant cash advance firms, commercial finance companies, equipment finance providers, working capital lenders, referral partners, underwriters, legal professionals, appraisers and insurers, where their involvement is reasonably necessary. They may independently verify banking and application information, request additional documents and make approval, decline, pricing and funding decisions. VFC does not control their underwriting criteria, fees, terms, conditions or funding timelines.

6. Other individuals

If providing information about a director, shareholder, spouse, guarantor, employee or other person, you must have lawful authority to provide it. We will obtain the individual's own consent where required, including for personal credit checks. Your signature alone does not authorize credit inquiries concerning another individual.

7. Electronic records and signatures

Where permitted, affirmative electronic acceptance or signature may record your consent. We may retain the applicable consent wording, version, date, time, identity and relevant technical audit information as evidence. You may request a copy or ask about alternative methods. This notice itself is not a signed financing or credit authorization. Materially new uses will be explained and fresh consent obtained where required.

8. Communications and marketing

We may contact you about your inquiry, application, account and requested service. Promotional email or text messages will be sent only on a lawful basis under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), with required sender identification, contact information and unsubscribe mechanism. Optional express marketing consent is separate from financing consent and is not a condition of service. You may unsubscribe at any time; requests will be implemented within ten business days where CASL applies. Necessary service communications may continue as permitted by law.

Authorized service communications may be made by phone, email, text, online portal or other agreed methods by VFC and relevant representatives or financing partners concerning missing documents, funding options, approvals, declines and requested renewals. Promotional renewal offers and related product marketing remain subject to applicable marketing consent requirements. Independent partners must establish their own lawful basis for promotional communications.

9. Storage, transfers and safeguards

Information may be processed or stored in Canada or other jurisdictions by service providers, subject to local laws and lawful government access. You may contact our Privacy Officer for information about relevant service providers and practices. We use reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards appropriate to sensitivity, including restricting access to authorized persons for legitimate purposes. No electronic transmission or storage is entirely risk-free; unauthorized access could cause financial loss, identity theft or adverse credit consequences. Please use approved secure upload methods rather than sending sensitive documents through social media or ordinary email.

10. Retention and disposal

We retain information only as long as necessary for identified purposes, legal obligations, legitimate dispute resolution and applicable recordkeeping requirements. Where BC PIPA requires it, information used to make a decision directly affecting an individual is retained for at least one year after use. When no longer required, information is securely destroyed or de-identified as appropriate, subject to lawful preservation obligations.

Records of declined, withdrawn or incomplete applications may be retained where reasonably necessary for identified lawful purposes, including compliance, fraud prevention, underwriting history and dispute resolution. Future financing reviews must remain within the scope of valid consent or other lawful authority.

11. Privacy incidents

We assess suspected privacy incidents, take reasonable steps to contain and mitigate them, maintain records as legally required, and notify affected individuals, regulators and other organizations when required by applicable law. Where PIPEDA applies, breaches presenting a real risk of significant harm must be reported and affected individuals notified as required.

12. Your rights and choices

You may request access to your personal information and information about its use and disclosure, request correction, challenge our practices or withdraw consent on reasonable notice, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. We will explain any effect on services. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing or compel deletion of legally required records. We may verify your identity and respond within applicable statutory deadlines, subject to lawful exceptions and permitted fees disclosed in advance. Contact independent lenders directly regarding information under their control.

13. Website and third-party links

Our website may use browser storage or cookies needed for functionality, authentication and security. Third-party websites and embedded services may have their own privacy practices. Any non-essential tracking that requires consent should be enabled only after the required choice is provided. Links to lender applications and social platforms are governed by those providers' terms and privacy policies. We do not treat browsing as consent to sensitive information processing.

14. Financing relationship

VFC facilitates financing as an intermediary where applicable. Approval, rates, fees, eligibility and terms are subject to the financing provider's independent assessment and due diligence. Provide accurate, current information and notify us of material changes. Regulated mortgage services are provided through the appropriately licensed brokerage identified to the client, with its own required disclosures and consent. Nothing here guarantees approval, creates a financing commitment or waives statutory rights.

Applicants confirm, to the best of their knowledge, that submitted information is true, accurate, complete and not misleading, and agree to promptly report material changes to ownership, operations, banking, credit, debts, income or financial circumstances. Material inaccuracies may result in a decline, cancellation, revised terms or other lawful lender remedies. No application guarantees funding, renewal, repayment amounts, pricing or timelines; all financing remains subject to documentation, final conditions and independent lender approval.

Responsibility for submitted information: The applicant is responsible for the authenticity, accuracy, completeness and lawful provision of information and documents submitted by or on behalf of the applicant. VFC may reasonably rely on information supplied by applicants and authorized third parties, subject to applicable verification and professional obligations. To the extent permitted by law, VFC is not responsible for losses, delays, declines or adverse decisions to the extent caused by false, incomplete, inaccurate, misleading, forged or unauthorized submissions or independent lender decisions outside VFC's control. This does not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or diminish VFC's privacy, security, accuracy, professional or statutory duties.

Artificial intelligence and model development

Where reasonably necessary to deliver requested financing services, VFC may use AI-assisted tools to extract and organize documents, analyze financial information, assess eligibility, identify potential risks and support underwriting and administrative workflows. Relevant information may be processed by authorized technology providers subject to appropriate safeguards. AI outputs may contain errors or bias; material findings should be checked by qualified personnel. Applicants may request human review of VFC assessments and correction of inaccurate information. Independent lenders make their own funding decisions.

VFC may use genuinely anonymized information for statistical analysis, testing and training or improving internal artificial intelligence models, provided individuals cannot reasonably be identified. Removing names alone is not sufficient to anonymize financial records. Pseudonymized or otherwise re-identifiable information remains personal information.

Use of identifiable personal information for optional AI model training, development or improvement beyond the requested financing service requires separate, informed, express opt-in consent before such use. The consent request must identify the specific training purpose, information categories, model operator, recipients, relevant storage jurisdictions, retention, foreseeable risks and withdrawal limitations. Refusal will not affect access to financing services. Raw credit reports, identification documents and bank statements must not be used for optional training unless the specific use is lawful, necessary and separately authorized, including any applicable provider restrictions. VFC must not permit external AI providers to independently train on client information without a lawful basis and appropriate specific authorization.

Optional training consent may be withdrawn for future use. Information will be removed from training datasets where required and feasible; any limitations concerning already-trained models must be explained before consent is obtained. No blanket waiver of privacy rights applies.
