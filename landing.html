Business Lines of Credit

$10,000 – $500,000

Access working capital as needed within your approved credit limit. Interest applies to the amount borrowed rather than the entire available limit.

Access funds as needed

Draw from your approved credit limit whenever working capital is required, subject to lender terms.

Flexible repayments

Fixed or variable weekly repayment options may be available.

Pay for what you use

Interest is generally charged on the outstanding amount borrowed, not the full available limit.

Flexible access to funds

With participating lenders, withdrawals may start at $500, with eligible Interac e-Transfer withdrawals available around the clock.

Repayment options

  • Fixed weekly payments: predictable installments recalculated after additional withdrawals.
  • Variable weekly payments: principal and interest payments that decrease as the balance is repaid, subject to lender terms.

Ideal for short-term needs

Inventory, payroll, repairs, cash-flow gaps, operating expenses and minor renovations.

Indicative eligibility

  • 6+ months in business.
  • $120,000+ annual revenue.
  • Credit score of 600 or higher.
  • Canadian incorporated business and business bank account.

BUSINESS FINANCING

Flex Funds Advance

$5,000 – $500,000

Receive upfront business capital in exchange for a portion of future sales receivables. Unlike a traditional loan, this advance has an agreed financing cost rather than interest.

Flexible funding

Access upfront capital based on your business sales and receivables.

Sales-aligned payments

Remittances may adjust with sales, depending on the agreement.

Clear financing cost

Review the agreed financing cost and total remittance amount upfront.

Repayments aligned with sales

With participating providers, remittances can be tied to a percentage of sales, so payments may decrease when revenue slows and increase when sales improve. Daily or weekly arrangements depend on the agreement.

Transparent financing cost

The agreed cost is established upfront. Review the total amount to be remitted and compare the cost with other financing options before committing.

Put your advance to work

  • Equipment, expansion and renovations.
  • Inventory, staffing and seasonal preparation.

Indicative eligibility

  • Typically at least 6 months in business.
  • Consistent sales and monthly revenue.
  • Recent bank statements, sales records and business information.

Sales performance is an important part of underwriting. An additional line of credit may be available subject to lender approval.
